Beyond the Halls

A hyper-realistic horror game inspired by the Backrooms, Dreamcore and the eerie found-footage style of Kane Pixels. Wander endless, mysterious levels, each packed with unique environments, strange phenomena and dangerous entities. Every hallway can lead somewhere unexpected, and reality may not always behave. Explore. Survive. Go beyond the halls.
